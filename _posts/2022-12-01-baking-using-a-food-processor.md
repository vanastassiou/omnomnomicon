---

You can adapt most small conventional bread, biscuit/scone, pie crust, and cake recipes (about 700 grams/1.5 pounds or less) to use a food processor as follows:

1.  Use the metal blade attachment, not the plastic one
2.  If using yeast, bloom it in some water
3.  Add dry ingredients to food processor bowl and pulse to combine
4.  If using oil or butter, add it and pulse until combined

-   -   For pie crust and other flaky pastries, stop when you get pea-sized bits of butter covered in flour

1.  Add cool liquid ingredients and:

-   -   For bread, pie crust, biscuits/scones, and shortbread-like cookies:

        -   Process just until a ball forms, then let machine run an additional 45 seconds and check dough consistency
        -   If it sticks to the blade, add 1 Tbsp flour and process 5 seconds
        -   If it's dry and crumbly, add 1 Tbsp liquid and process 5 seconds
