---

- Boxed brownie or chocolate cake mix enhancers:
  - Brewed coffee instead of water
  - A squirt of Hershey's chocolate syrup
- Boxed cake mix enhancers:
  - One can of pop to replace all ingredients to be added
  - One can of pumpkin pie mix to replace all other ingredients
  - Any flavourful liquid instead of water
  - Browned butter or other flavoured oil instead of vegetable oil
  - Add one box pudding mix
  - Extra egg
- Cookies:
  - Swap out 1/4 volume of the butter for an equal volume of cream cheese
  - Add vanilla pudding mix to dough
  - Swap out ¼ volume of flour with cornmeal
- Pie crust:
  - One block cream cheese with enough flour mixed in to get the right consistency (about 1-1/2 cups) makes one 9" pie shell
- Icing:
  - Cream cheese mixed into canned buttercream frosting
