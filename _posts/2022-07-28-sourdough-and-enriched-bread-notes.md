## 1. sourdough bread with dough softener (fat)

- all-purpose flour is fine; adjust hydration accordingly.
- 100% hydration starter works without major changes; adjust water to compensate.
- for sandwich loaves, bake in a greased 9x5 or 10x5 inch pan.
- cast iron pans can be used but need oiling; silicone pans are okay but less crisp.
- dough softeners include butter, oil, milk, sugar, eggs.
- add fat after autolyse, before bulk ferment.

## 3. process overview for enriched sourdough breads

1. mix flour, water, starter  
   - add sugar if ≤10% baker’s ratio  
2. autolyse 30–45 minutes  
3. add salt and enrichment (fat, eggs, milk)  
4. develop gluten (knead or folds)  
   - staggered sugar addition if ≥10% sugar  
5. bulk ferment 4–8 hrs with folds  
6. shape and final proof (2–5 hrs or overnight cold)  
7. bake at 180–220°C for 20–45 minutes depending on loaf type  

---

## 4. when to add sugar and fat

- sugar: with initial mix if ≤10%, otherwise stagger after gluten forms  
- fat: after autolyse, with salt, before bulk ferment  

---

## 5. waking old starter

- discard most, keep ~30 g  
- feed 1:2:2 with lukewarm water  
- feed every 12 hrs until doubling in 4–6 hrs  
- add rye or whole wheat flour to boost if needed  

---

## 6. use of candied orange peel and syrup

- peel: 5–15% of flour weight, chopped  
- syrup: replace part of sugar and liquid; reduce water accordingly  
- syrup best for enriched doughs and glazes

## 7. recipes provided

### cinnamon raisin sourdough (30% whole wheat)

#### ingredients

- 263 g all-purpose flour
- 112 g whole wheat flour
- 275–285 g water (73–76% hydration)
- 90 g active 100% hydration starter (24%)
- 30 g sugar (8%)
- 30 g butter or oil (8%)
- 7 g salt (1.8%)
- 100 g raisins
- 1.5 tsp cinnamon

#### process

1. Mix flours, water, and starter. Rest 45 min (autolyse).
2. Add salt and butter. Mix until cohesive.
3. Bulk ferment 4–5 hrs at room temp. Do 3–4 folds.
4. Add raisins and cinnamon during 2nd or 3rd fold.
5. Shape into log and place in greased 9x5” loaf pan.
6. Final proof 2–3 hrs at room temp or overnight in fridge.
7. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 35–40 min, internal temp ~93°C (200°F).
