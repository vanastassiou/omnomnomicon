---
title: "Vegan egg substitutes for baking"
date: 2022-04-18
excerpt: "Egg replacements for baking, organized by function: binding, leavening, whipping, thickening, and flavour."
---


-   One whole large egg (for binding):

    -   2 Tbsp baking powder + 2 Tbsp water + 1 tsp oil
    -   ¼ cup pureed banana, prune, apricot, apple, or pear
    -   1 Tbsp ground flaxseed (or chia) soaked in 3 Tbsp hot water
    -   1 Tbsp soy/chickpea flour soaked in 3 Tbsp water

-   One whole large egg (for leavening):

    -   ¼ cup seltzer, or 1:1 substitute for all liquid (fold in gently)
    -   Any of the "binding" substitutes + ½ tsp baking powder

-   Egg whites (for whipping/leavening):

    -   Chickpea canning liquid can be whipped to a meringue
    -   1 Tbsp agar powder in 1 Tbsp water, chilled and whipped

-   Egg yolks (for thickening):

    -   ¼ cup pureed silken tofu, vegan yogurt, or avocado
    -   1 Tbsp soy/chickpea flour or cornstarch
    -   1 Tbsp agar powder in 3 Tbsp water

-   Egg yolks (for flavour):

    -   1 Tbsp any fat with the desired flavour
