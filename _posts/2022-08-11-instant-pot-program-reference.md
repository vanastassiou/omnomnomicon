## Pressure reference

- Working pressure
  - Low: 5.8-7.2 psi (40-50 kPa)
  - High: 10.2-11.6 psi (70-80 kPa)
- **Steam release pressure limit:** 16.68 psi (115 kPa)

## Pressure cooking presets

"Low" and "high" pressure options are usually available in these presets. The Less, Normal, and More columns are the cook times for the Adjust key's three settings.

| Preset | Less | Normal | More |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soup | Smart program to prevent rolling boil; adjust cook time as desired | | |
| Porridge | 15 min cook | 20 min cook | 30 min cook |
| Poultry | 5 min cook | 15 min cook | 30 min cook |
| Meat/Stew | 20 min cook | 35 min cook | 45 min cook |
| Bean/Chili | | 30 min cook | 40 min cook |
| Rice | Smart program; cook time depends on amount of food (usually 12-14 min); "Adjust" key has no effect | | |
| Multigrain | 20 min cook | 40 min cook | 45 min warm soak + 60 min cook |
| Steam | Uses max heat through entire cooking cycle; put food in steamer basket over 1-2 cups water; for vegetables, steam 1-2 min with quick release | | |
| Manual | Sets cooking time manually (max 240 min); timer begins when working pressure is reached | | |

## No-pressure presets

Cook times are manually adjustable. The Less, Normal, and More columns are the temperature ranges for the Adjust key's three settings.

| Preset | Less | Normal | More |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yogurt | 30-34°C (86-93.2°F) | 36-43°C (96.8-109.4°F) | 71-83°C (160-180°F) |
| Keep Warm | 63-78°C (145-172°F) | | |
| Slow Cook | 180-190°F | 190-200°F | 200-210°F |
| Saute | 275-302°F | 320-349°F | 347-410°F |

## Manual settings for various uses

| Use | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eggs | **Soft**: *Manual* 4 min on High | **Medium**: *Manual* 5 min on High | **Hard**: *Manual* 6 min on High |
| Cake | **Light**: *Manual* 25 min on High | **Denser**: *Manual* 40 min on High | **Very dense**: *Manual* 50 min on High |
| Sanitize | **Pasteurizing milk**: *Yogurt* on More | **Sterilizing utensils**: *Steam* 5 minutes | **Water bath canning**: *Steam* for amount of processing time indicated by recipe |
