title: "Vegan dairy substitutes"
date: 2022-01-05
excerpt: "Plant-based swaps for butter, milk, and whipping cream in baking and cooking."
---

## Butter

-   In cakes

    -   Vegetable shortening (not margarine) in 1:1 ratio

    -   Replace completely with 2/3 of its volume in vegetable oil

        -   Replace 1:1 if reducing other liquid in recipe

-   In quick breads: replace up to half the butter with applesauce or another fruit puree in a complementary flavour

## Milk

-   Any nut, grain, or legume milk, or other neutral liquid
-   Any unsweetened juice with a mild flavour

## Cream for Whipping

-   Chill a can of coconut cream and scoop out the separated cream
-   Grind raw cashews, then puree with an equal volume of canned pear and chill well
-   Combine ½ cup canning liquid from chickpeas, 1/8 tsp cream of tartar, and 2/3 cup powdered sugar, then chill well
