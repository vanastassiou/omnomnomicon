_Serves 6._

### Make the custard

1. Set a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 325°F (163°C). Arrange six ½-cup (120 ml) ramekins in a large baking dish.
2. In a small saucepan, combine the cream with the vanilla seeds and pod. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
3. Remove from the heat and whisk in the vanilla extract.
4. In a teakettle, bring 4 cups (960 ml) water to a boil, then remove from the heat.
5. In a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, whip the egg yolks, ⅓ cup (50 g) of the sugar, and the salt on medium until pale yellow, about 2 minutes.
6. Discard the vanilla pod from the cream. With the mixer on medium-low, slowly stream in the hot cream, scraping down the bowl as needed.

### Bake

1. Divide the mixture evenly among the ramekins.
2. Carefully pour enough hot water into the baking dish to come about halfway up the sides of the ramekins; you may not use all the water.
3. Bake until set but slightly wobbly in the centre, 45 to 50 minutes.
4. Remove the ramekins from the water bath and cool for about 30 minutes at room temperature, then refrigerate, covered, for at least 2 hours or overnight.

### Brûlée the tops

1. At least 30 minutes before serving, set a rack close to the oven's heat source and heat the broiler.
2. Sprinkle the remaining 2 tablespoons sugar in a thin, even layer over each custard, tapping out any excess.
3. Broil until the sugar is evenly caramelized, 5 to 8 minutes. Let cool for at least 15 minutes before serving. (It can also be served chilled: cool for about 30 minutes, then refrigerate until ready to serve.)
