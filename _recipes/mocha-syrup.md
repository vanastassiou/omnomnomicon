  - name: sweetened natural cocoa powder

  - name: boiling water
    amount: 120
    unit: mL

_Makes about 240 mL._

1. In a small heatproof bowl, combine cocoa powder and boiling water.
2. Whisk until cocoa is fully incorporated and there are no lumps.
3. Store refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 days.
