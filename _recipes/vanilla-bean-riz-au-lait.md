_Serves 4._

### Cook the rice

1. In a medium saucepan, combine milk and heavy cream. Scrape in vanilla seeds and add pods.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
3. Add rice, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer, stirring occasionally, until rice is tender, about 30 minutes.

### Finish

1. Discard vanilla pods and add brown sugar and vanilla extract. Simmer, stirring frequently, until as thick as Greek yogurt, about 10 minutes.
2. Remove from heat and stir in more milk as desired for a looser consistency.
3. Spoon into small cups or bowls and, if desired, garnish with cardamom, cinnamon, or dulce de leche.
