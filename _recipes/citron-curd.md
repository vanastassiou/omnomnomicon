_Makes about 3 cups (690 g); the filling for one Tarte au Citron._

1. In a medium saucepan, combine the milk and lemon juice. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and sugar until combined. Whisk in the cornstarch until fully incorporated.
3. Whisking vigorously, gradually add ¼ cup (60 ml) of the hot milk. Repeat with another ¼ cup (60 ml), then slowly add the rest, whisking so the eggs don't scramble.
4. Return the mixture to the saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, whisking constantly, until as thick as pudding and bubbling slightly, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. Remove from the heat and whisk until smooth, then add the butter one piece at a time, whisking until fully incorporated.
