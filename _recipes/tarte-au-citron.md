_Makes one 9½-inch (24 cm) tart._

### Assemble

1. Pour warm citron curd into cooled tart shell and spread it evenly to edges with an offset spatula.
2. Place a piece of plastic wrap directly on surface and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight to set.

### Serve

1. Carefully remove plastic wrap.
2. Dollop with whipped cream, decorate with lemon slices, and sprinkle with lemon zest, if desired. Enjoy immediately.
