_Serves 4 to 6._

### Prepare the mushrooms and stock

1. Simmer the dried mushrooms in the stock in a saucepan for 5 minutes.
2. Remove the mushrooms from the broth and roughly chop.
3. Add the fresh mushroom trimmings to the hot stock and set aside.

### Build the risotto

1. Set the pressure cooker to Sauté mode, add the oil and butter, and heat until the mixture stops foaming.
2. Add the fresh mushrooms, season with salt and pepper, and cook until browned.
3. Add the onion, garlic, and chopped reconstituted mushrooms, and cook until fragrant.
4. Stir in the rice and cook until slightly translucent and nutty-smelling.
5. Stir in the soy sauce and miso paste, then add the wine and cook until it is almost fully evaporated.
6. Strain the stock into the pot and discard the mushroom scraps.

### Pressure cook and finish

1. Seal the pressure cooker and cook 5 minutes on low pressure.
2. Quick-release the pressure, open the cooker, and stir together the rice and cooking liquid.
3. Stir in the cheese, herbs, and cream (if using).
4. Season to taste with salt and pepper, and serve immediately.

### Notes

- The finished risotto should be thick enough to slowly refill a trail dragged through it with a spoon. If too thin, cook a bit longer, stirring regularly; if too thick, stir in a little more broth or water.
- This recipe can be done in a Dutch oven using a similar process, but cooking will take about 30 minutes.
