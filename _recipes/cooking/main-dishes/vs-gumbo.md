_Serves 4._

### Brown the meats

1. In a skillet, brown the sausage well and remove with a slotted spoon.
2. Add the chicken to the skillet, adding oil as needed to keep it from sticking, and remove with a slotted spoon once browned.
3. Deglaze the skillet with a little stock and reserve for later.

### Make the roux and base

1. In a large pot, heat the oil until smoking hot, then add the flour and cook, stirring frequently, until the colour of milk chocolate.
2. Add the chopped celery, onion, pepper, and garlic, and cook until lightly browned and translucent.
3. Add the okra and cook until it softens.
4. Add the Cajun seasoning and cook until fragrant.

### Simmer and finish

1. Add the chicken, sausage, beer, stock, tomato sauce, and deglazing liquid, and stir well.
2. Cover and simmer gently about 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
3. Add the seafood and parsley just before serving and cook only until just heated through, about 10 minutes at a simmer.

### Notes

- Roux can be frozen for use in other recipes, so err on the side of making more than you need.
- Don't shorten the simmering time; it is necessary for the flavours of the spices to mature.
- Serve over steamed rice to extend this recipe to 6 servings.
