_Makes one 9x5-inch (23x13 cm) loaf; serves about 10._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a loaf tin.
2. Mix all ingredients in a bowl, then pour the mixture into the tin.
3. Bake until set, about 40 minutes.
4. Unmould the loaf onto a baking sheet, glaze with ketchup, and broil for 5 to 7 minutes.
