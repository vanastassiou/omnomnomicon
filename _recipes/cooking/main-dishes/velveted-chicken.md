_Serves 2._

1. Combine the baking soda and chicken, and let stand 20 minutes.
2. Rinse and drain the chicken.
3. Add seasonings of your choice.
4. Stir fry as desired.

### Notes

- Marinating overnight in seasonings drastically improves flavour.
