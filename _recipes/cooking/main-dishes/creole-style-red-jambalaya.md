_Serves 8._

### Prepare the liquid

1. Strain the chunks out of the tomatoes, adding the juice to a 4-cup measure and reserving the chunks for later.
2. Add enough chicken stock to the tomato juice to make 4 cups (about 950 mL).
3. Preheat the oven to 325°F (160°C).

### Brown and build

1. Add oil to an oven-safe pot, then brown the chicken well on the stovetop.
2. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board, let rest 5 minutes, then cut into small chunks.
3. Add the sausage to the pot and brown.
4. Add the chopped vegetables and cook until lightly browned.
5. Add the tomato paste and cook until browned.
6. Add the spices, hot sauce, reserved tomatoes, and chicken, seasoning with salt to taste. Season slightly aggressively, since the rice will dilute the flavour as it absorbs the broth.

### Bake

1. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then stir in the rice.
2. Cover the pot with an oven-safe lid and transfer to the oven.
3. Bake until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender, about 40 minutes.
4. Remove the pot from the oven, fold in the shrimp and scallions, and cover.
5. Return the pot to the oven until the shrimp are just cooked through.
6. Remove from the oven and let rest 15 minutes before serving.
