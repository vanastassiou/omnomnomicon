_Makes one 9x5-inch loaf; serves about 8._

1. Combine all ingredients except the ketchup in a bowl and mix well.
2. Let the mixture sit 10 minutes.
3. Line a 9x5-inch loaf tin with aluminum foil and pack the mixture into the tin.
4. Spread the ketchup over the top of the loaf.
5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) about 50 minutes.
6. Let cool in the pan about 15 minutes, then unmould, slice, and serve.
