_Serves 6._

### Prepare the beans

1. Soak the beans overnight in salted water.
2. Drain and rinse the beans.

### Brown and build

1. In a large Dutch oven, heat the duck fat and brown all the meats thoroughly in batches, removing each with a slotted spoon and setting aside.
2. Add the onion to the pot and cook until translucent, stirring well.
3. Add all remaining ingredients and simmer, covered, until the beans are tender, about 45 minutes.
4. Remove and discard the vegetables, herbs, and spices.
5. Stir the reserved meat into the pot, arranging the chicken pieces on top of the beans with the skin facing up; the beans should be almost completely submerged in broth.

### Braise

1. Transfer to the oven and cook, uncovered, at 300°F (150°C) for about 2 hours.
2. Break the gelatin crust with a spoon and shake the pot gently to redistribute; add water as necessary to keep the beans barely covered.
3. Repeat the break-and-shake process about every 30 minutes over the next 2 hours.
4. Let cook undisturbed for another hour or two, until the crust is deep brown and thick.
5. Serve immediately with fresh bread.
