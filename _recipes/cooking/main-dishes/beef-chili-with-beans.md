_Serves 10._

### Brown the meat

1. Season the meat generously with salt and pepper.
2. Add oil to a large, heavy-bottomed pot and brown the meat well on all sides, reserving any rendered fat separately.
3. Transfer the meat to a cutting board, cut into bite-sized pieces, and add any juices to the chile purée.
4. Deglaze the pot with 1 cup of the stock, then stir the liquid into the chile purée.

### Build the chili

1. Add 4 tablespoons (60 mL) oil or reserved beef fat to the pot and heat well.
2. Add the onions, garlic, fresh chiles, and oregano, and cook until fragrant and translucent.
3. Stir in the chile purée and cook until the mixture thickens.
4. Add the remaining stock, meat, and bay leaves, and bring to a simmer.
5. Add the beans and simmer about 1 hour with the lid slightly ajar.
6. Add the crushed tomatoes and cider vinegar, and cook until the beans and beef are fully tender and the broth is rich and lightly thickened, about 2½ more hours.

### Finish

1. Remove and discard the bay leaves and any bones.
2. Stir in the vodka or bourbon and the hot sauce.
3. Season to taste with salt, pepper, and additional vinegar as desired.

### Notes

- To cook in a pressure cooker: add all ingredients from the "Build the chili" phase to the cooker, cook on high pressure for 40 minutes, and let the pressure release naturally. If the sauce is too thin, simmer gently to reduce.
- This chili tastes better if refrigerated overnight and reheated.
