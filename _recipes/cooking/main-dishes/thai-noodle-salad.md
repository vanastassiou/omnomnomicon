_Serves 6._

### Prepare the noodles

1. Cook the noodles according to the package directions.
2. Drain the noodles and rinse with cold water.

### Assemble

1. Mix together the dressing: Thai peanut sauce, lime juice, and fish sauce.
2. Add all the vegetables to a large salad bowl and mix well.
3. Add the cold noodles and toss lightly to avoid breaking them.
4. Add the dressing and toss to combine.
5. Adjust to taste and garnish with salt, lime, cilantro, and crushed peanuts.

### Notes

- For the dressing, use a pre-made Thai-style peanut sauce or the recipe in this book. Vietnamese-style peanut sauce is based on hoisin and too sweet, but you can mix it 2:1 with sriracha to bring it in the right direction.
- Use chiffonade-cut mint or Thai basil if cilantro isn't an option.
- This salad keeps well in the fridge overnight if made with broad rice noodles.
- Add sliced, grilled proteins to turn this salad into a meal.
