_Serves 6._

### Make the custard

1. Set a rack in center of oven and preheat to 325°F (163°C). Arrange six 120 mL ramekins in a large baking dish.
2. In a small saucepan, combine cream with vanilla seeds and pod. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
3. Remove from heat and whisk in vanilla extract.
4. In a teakettle, bring 960 mL water to a boil, then remove from heat.
5. In a stand mixer fitted with whisk attachment, whip egg yolks, 50 g of sugar, and salt on medium until pale yellow, about 2 minutes.
6. Discard vanilla pod from cream. With mixer on medium-low, slowly stream in hot cream, scraping down bowl as needed.

### Bake

1. Divide mixture evenly among ramekins.
2. Carefully pour enough hot water into baking dish to come about halfway up sides of ramekins; you may not use all water.
3. Bake until set but slightly wobbly in centre, 45 to 50 minutes.
4. Remove ramekins from water bath and cool for about 30 minutes at room temperature, then refrigerate, covered, for at least 2 hours or overnight.

### Brûlée the tops

1. At least 30 minutes before serving, set a rack close to oven's heat source and heat broiler.
2. Sprinkle remaining 2 tablespoons sugar in a thin, even layer over each custard, tapping out any excess.
3. Broil until sugar is evenly caramelized, 5 to 8 minutes. Let cool for at least 15 minutes before serving. (It can also be served chilled: cool for about 30 minutes, then refrigerate until ready to serve.)
