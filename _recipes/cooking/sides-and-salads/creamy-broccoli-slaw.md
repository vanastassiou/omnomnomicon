_Serves 6._

1. Trim the broccoli and cut it into large chunks, then chop it down into smaller pieces and thin slices.
2. Toss the broccoli with the almonds, cranberries, and red onion.
3. Blend the buttermilk, mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, and shallot into a smooth dressing, then season with salt and pepper.
4. Combine the dressing with the broccoli mixture and toss to coat.

### Notes

- If the broccoli has large stems, peel off the tough skin, then slice the tender core thinly to add to the salad.
- A food processor's slicing disk makes quick work of the broccoli and onion; switch to the chopping blade to blend the dressing thoroughly.
- No buttermilk? An equal volume of yogurt or sour cream substitutes well.
