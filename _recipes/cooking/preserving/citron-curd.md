_Makes about 690 g; filling for one Tarte au Citron._

1. In a medium saucepan, combine milk and lemon juice. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together egg yolks and sugar until combined. Whisk in cornstarch until fully incorporated.
3. Whisking vigorously, gradually add 60 mL of hot milk. Repeat with another 60 mL, then slowly add rest, whisking so eggs don't scramble.
4. Return mixture to saucepan and cook over medium-low heat, whisking constantly, until as thick as pudding and bubbling slightly, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. Remove from heat and whisk until smooth, then add butter one piece at a time, whisking until fully incorporated.
