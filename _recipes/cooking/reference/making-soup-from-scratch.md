_Serves 4._

This is a formula rather than a fixed recipe: hold the ratios loosely and swap ingredients to suit preference or supply.

1. In a soup pot, cook the aromatics with a pinch of salt in the fat until soft.
2. Add the spices and cook a little longer to bloom their aroma.
3. Add the protein, liquid, and vegetables, then simmer until everything is softened. Use roughly 1 cup chopped meat, 2 cups cooked beans, or 60 mL nut butter, depending on which protein you choose.
4. For a creamy soup, purée with an immersion blender at this point.
5. Ladle into bowls and add garnishes, if using.

### Notes

- Play with the proportions according to preference or supply.
- Browning the aromatics and meat first increases flavour.
