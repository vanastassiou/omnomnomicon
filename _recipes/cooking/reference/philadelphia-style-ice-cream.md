_Makes about 1.5 L._

1. Combine the cream, milk, sugar, corn syrup, and salt in a saucepan and heat, stirring, until the sugar dissolves.
2. Chill completely, at least 4 hours and preferably overnight.
3. Churn in an ice cream maker according to the manufacturer's directions.

### Notes

- Steep in the hot mixture for 30 minutes, then strain:
  - Vanilla: 2 vanilla beans, sliced in half and scraped.
  - Coffee: 120 mL whole coffee beans, coarsely ground.
  - Green tea: 60 mL green tea leaves.
- Herbs, spices, or citrus: blend with the sugar, then steep 30 minutes:
  - Tender herbs: 240 mL clean, dry leaves.
  - Cinnamon: one 10 cm (4-inch) cinnamon stick.
  - Lemon or lime: zest of 3; add the juice of 1 before chilling.
- Soft fruits: make the base without the milk, then cook the fruit with sugar and blend smooth; add to the base before chilling:
  - Berries: 450 g berries, 45 mL sugar, 2.5 mL lemon juice.
  - Stone fruits: 680 g fruit, 30 to 60 mL sugar, 120 mL crème fraîche (or sour cream), and 15 mL amaretto.
- Chocolate: reduce the base to 360 mL cream and 180 mL milk, then:
  - Add 5 mL Dutch-process cocoa powder and 170 g chopped chocolate to the hot base; let it melt and stir smooth.
  - Add 180 mL crème fraîche and 5 mL vanilla extract.
- Nuts: make the base with 600 mL milk, 360 mL cream, and 30 mL corn syrup; then whisk in 240 mL nut paste and 5 mL vanilla extract; add sugar to taste.
  - Variation: make the base using 120 mL toasted chopped nuts simmered in and strained out; mix in 120 mL more.
  - Coconut: follow the variation with shredded coconut, but replace 240 mL of the cream with coconut milk or cream.
- Salted caramel: caramelize 150 g sugar in a saucepan, then add the milk, cream, 50 g sugar, and 60 mL corn syrup; add 1.25 mL sea salt near the end of the churn.
