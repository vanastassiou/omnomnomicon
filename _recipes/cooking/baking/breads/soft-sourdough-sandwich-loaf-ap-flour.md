_Makes one loaf._

1. Mix the flour, water, starter, and sugar.
2. Autolyse 30 to 45 minutes.
3. Add the salt, oil, and gluten.
4. Knead, or stretch and fold, until elastic.
5. Bulk ferment 4 to 5 hours with folds.
6. Shape and place in a greased 10x5 pan.
7. Final proof 2 to 4 hours, or overnight.
8. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 40 to 45 minutes, until golden and 93°C (199°F) internal.
