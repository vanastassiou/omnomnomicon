_Makes one loaf._

1. Mix the flour, gluten, water, starter, and sugar.
2. Autolyse 30 to 45 minutes.
3. Add the salt and oil.
4. Knead until smooth.
5. Fold in the cinnamon and raisins gently.
6. Bulk ferment 4 to 5 hours with 2 folds.
7. Shape into a loaf and proof 2 to 4 hours, or overnight.
8. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for 40 to 45 minutes.
