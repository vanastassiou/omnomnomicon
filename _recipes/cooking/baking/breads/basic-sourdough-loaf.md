_Makes one 9x5 loaf._

1. Combine all ingredients in a large bowl and mix until all the flour has been absorbed.
2. Pat the dough into a ball, cover with plastic wrap, and let rise until doubled.
3. Turn the dough out of the bowl and place it into a loaf pan lined with parchment paper.
4. Let rise until a dent made with your finger fills back in slowly. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 400°F (205°C).
5. Make a shallow slash down the middle of the loaf.
6. Bake about 45 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 205°F (96°C).
7. Remove the bread from the pan and let it cool completely on a rack before cutting.

### Notes

- For a taller loaf, stretch and fold the dough twice during the first rise: pull one section of the dough ball and fold it back into the middle, tucking in the end; give the bowl a quarter turn and repeat until you have folded 4 times total.
- For extra-crusty bread, put a pan of boiling water in the oven to generate steam during baking.
- For larger bubbles in the crumb, increase the water to 300 mL.
- To use all-purpose flour instead, apply the same technique with 50 g active sourdough starter, 330 mL warm water, 9 g salt, and 500 g all-purpose flour.
