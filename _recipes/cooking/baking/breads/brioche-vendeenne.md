_Makes 2 loaves._

1. Soak the raisins in the brandy for 45 minutes, then drain the brandy into a mixing bowl and set the raisins aside.
2. Add half the flour plus the salt and yeast to the bowl of a stand mixer.
3. Separately, add the eggs, melted butter, and sugar to the bowl holding the brandy and whip together.
4. With the mixer running on slow speed and fitted with the dough hook, slowly add the egg mixture to the flour mixture, scraping down the bowl as needed.
5. Add more flour 60 mL (1/2 cup) at a time until the dough forms a ball around the hook, then knead an additional 10 minutes.
6. Let the dough rise, covered with plastic wrap, until doubled, about 3 hours.
7. Punch down the dough and let it rise another 2 hours, until doubled.
8. Knead the raisins into the dough, then shape it into a braid.
9. Place the loaf on a baking sheet and let it rise another hour, until doubled.
10. Brush the dough with the egg wash.
11. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 to 40 minutes, until the loaf sounds hollow when tapped.
12. Dust with icing sugar before serving.

### Notes

- To use a food processor: pulse to blend during step 4; process during step 5 only until the dough forms a ball that rides on the blade; process the dough in batches if the ingredients do not all fit at once; then knead all the dough together for 45 seconds.
