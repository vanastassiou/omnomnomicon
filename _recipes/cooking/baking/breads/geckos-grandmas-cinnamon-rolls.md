_Makes 9 rolls._

### Dough

1. Combine all the dough ingredients and let rise, covered, in a warm place until doubled, about 30 minutes.

### Filling and shaping

1. Combine the filling ingredients in a small bowl.
2. Roll the dough out into a rectangle roughly 9x13 inches.
3. Spread the filling evenly over the dough, leaving about 1/2 inch bare along one long edge.
4. Roll the dough up loosely, starting from one short edge, to get a 9 inch log.
5. Using unflavoured dental floss or a serrated knife, cut the log into nine 1 inch slices.
6. Arrange the slices cut side down on a baking sheet, tucking each one's tail of dough underneath to prevent unravelling.

### Bake and finish

1. Let the rolls rise until the dough does not immediately spring back when pushed with a fingertip. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Bake the rolls until light golden brown, about 15 minutes; the internal temperature should reach 190°F (88°C).
3. Brush the rolls with the reserved melted butter.
4. If using icing, whisk the icing ingredients together and let the rolls cool 10 to 15 minutes before applying it.

### Notes

- If planning to store or freeze the rolls, do not ice them; wait until you plan to serve them.
- For cinnamon rolls that are less crusty, arrange them in a 9x9 baking pan and brush them with melted butter before baking.
