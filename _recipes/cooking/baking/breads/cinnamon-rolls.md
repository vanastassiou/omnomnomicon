_Makes 9 buns in one 9x9 pan._

### Dough

1. Mix the milk, yeast, sugar, egg, egg yolk, and melted butter in the bowl of a stand mixer.
2. Add the flour and salt, then knead with the dough hook until a ball forms.
3. Transfer the dough to an oiled bowl and cover with plastic wrap.
4. Let rise until doubled in size, 1 to 1.5 hours.

### Assemble

1. Roll the dough out into a 14x9 inch rectangle.
2. Spread the softened filling butter over the dough, leaving 0.25 inch bare at the far side.
3. Mix the brown sugar and cinnamon, then spread the mixture over the butter.
4. Roll the dough up, starting from the short side.
5. Cut the roll into 1 inch sections using a serrated knife.
6. Place the rolls in a lined and greased 9x9 baking pan.
7. Cover the pan with plastic wrap and let rise 30 to 45 minutes.

### Bake and frost

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and bake the rolls 20 to 25 minutes, until slightly golden brown at the edges.
2. Let cool 5 to 10 minutes.
3. Beat the cream cheese, softened frosting butter, icing sugar, and vanilla extract until smooth and fluffy.
4. Spread the frosting over the slightly cooled rolls.

### Notes

- To make ahead: follow the dough and assembly steps through placing the rolls in the pan, then cover tightly and refrigerate up to 48 hours before resuming.
- The frosting can be made up to 3 days ahead if refrigerated, or a month ahead if frozen. Let it come to room temperature and whip until smooth before using.
