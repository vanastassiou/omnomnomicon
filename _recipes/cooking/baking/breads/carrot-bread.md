_Makes one 9x5 loaf._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9x5 loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. Mix the wet ingredients together in a large bowl.
3. Whisk together the dry ingredients separately.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix just until combined.
5. Pour into the pan and bake about 60 minutes.

### Notes

- Wrapped well, a loaf lasts about 1 week at room temperature or 1 month in the freezer.
- If you do not have any pie spice blend, use a combination of ginger, nutmeg, cloves, and cinnamon, or even just cinnamon.
