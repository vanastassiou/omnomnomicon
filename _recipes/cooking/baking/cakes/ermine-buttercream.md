_Makes about 3 cups, enough to frost one 8-inch cake layer._

1. Combine the flour, milk, and sugar in a saucepan and simmer, whisking continuously, until very thick.
2. Let the mixture cool to room temperature.
3. Using a stand mixer, beat the butter on high speed until very fluffy.
4. With the mixer still running, add the vanilla, salt, and flour mixture one spoonful at a time.
5. Beat until the icing reaches the consistency of whipped cream.

### Notes

- For a chocolate frosting, add about 30 g cocoa powder when cooking the pudding.
- Frost your cake quickly or be prepared to re-whip the icing, as it sets quickly.
- Like all buttercream icing, this can be refrigerated or frozen in an airtight container for later. To use, let it come to room temperature and then re-whip until fluffy again.
