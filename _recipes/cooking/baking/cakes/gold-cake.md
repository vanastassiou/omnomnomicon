_Makes three 9-inch cake layers._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C), then grease and line the cake pans.
2. In a small bowl, mix together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. In an electric mixer bowl, beat the yolks, vanilla, lemon juice, and lemon zest until fluffy, then transfer the mixture to another bowl.
4. Cream the butter and sugar until very airy and fluffy.
5. Add the egg mixture to the butter and beat until homogeneous.
6. Reduce the mixer speed to low, then alternate adding the flour mixture and milk, mixing each addition until just combined.
7. Divide the batter between the pans and smooth it with a spatula.
8. Bake about 18 to 20 minutes.
9. Let cool 10 minutes in the pans, then unmould onto a rack and let cool completely.
