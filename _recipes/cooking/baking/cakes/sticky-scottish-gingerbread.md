_Makes one 9-inch square cake._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C), then grease and line an 8 to 9-inch square baking pan or a 9 to 10-inch round cake pan.
2. Put the butter, sugar, treacle, and spices in a large saucepan and heat gently, stirring until the butter is just melted and fully incorporated.
3. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the buttermilk.
4. Once the mixture has cooled to body temperature, stir in the eggs.
5. Whisk the flour and baking soda together, then add to the pan and stir in.
6. Mix in the chopped candied ginger.
7. Pour the mixture into the pan and bake 45 to 60 minutes; test by pressing on the top, which should spring back easily when ready.
8. Let cool in the pan and store in an airtight container overnight before serving, for best flavour and texture.

### Notes

- If you have no black treacle, use an equal weight of molasses (any type).
- If you have no buttermilk, use plain yogurt or milk soured with 15 mL lemon juice.
