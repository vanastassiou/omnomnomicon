_Serves 16, one 8-inch three-layer cake._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C), then line and grease three 8x3 cake pans.
2. Sift the flour and cocoa together and set aside.
3. Add the butter, brown sugar, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and vanilla extract to the bowl of a stand mixer and cream until light and fluffy.
4. Add the eggs, beating each one in completely before adding the next.
5. Reduce the speed to low and alternate adding the flour and cocoa mixture and the red wine in one-third portions.
6. Divide the batter among the prepared cake pans, about 22 ounces each.
7. Bake until the cakes pass the toothpick test, then remove from the oven.
8. Let the cakes cool completely, then remove from the pans and peel off the parchment.
9. Crumb-coat, layer, and frost the cake.

### Notes

- Covered, the frosted cake keeps 24 hours at room temperature.
- Store individually wrapped leftover slices at room temperature up to 3 additional days.
- Red devil cake: replace the red wine with an equal weight of cultured buttermilk at room temperature.
- Alcohol-free: replace the red wine with an equal weight of unsweetened cranberry juice.
- Gluten-free: replace the all-purpose flour with 115 g teff flour, 115 g tapioca flour or arrowroot, 60 g coconut flour, and 60 g white rice flour.
