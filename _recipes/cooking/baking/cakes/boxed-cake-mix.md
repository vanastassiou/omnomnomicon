_Makes one 18.25 oz cake mix, enough for two 9-inch rounds._

1. Combine all the dry ingredients in an electric mixer bowl.
2. Add the shortening and blend until it disappears into the mix.

### Notes

- For a white cake, beat together and bake at 350°F (175°C):
  - 1 recipe boxed cake mix (about 700 g)
  - 320 mL water
  - 120 mL white shortening or lard
  - 4 egg whites
  - 5 mL vanilla
- For a yellow cake, follow the white cake steps except:
  - Use 3 whole eggs instead of the egg whites.
  - Use an equal amount of butter instead of shortening or lard.
- Chocolate cake mix variation:
  - Replace about 95 g of the flour with an equal volume of cocoa powder.
  - Add 5 mL baking soda.
- To bake the chocolate cake, follow the yellow cake steps except:
  - Use the chocolate cake mix variation.
  - Replace the butter with an equal volume of vegetable oil.
- When baking the cake, you can replace the water and fat with 500 mL melted high-quality ice cream, but you will still need eggs.
- For a dairy-free cake, omit the milk powder, then replace the water with a non-dairy milk when baking.
