_Makes one 9x5 loaf, about 10 slices._

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C), then grease and line a 9x5 loaf pan.
2. In the bowl of an electric mixer, beat the butter, sugar, and zests until light and fluffy, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the eggs one at a time, then half the marmalade and the orange juice, scraping down the bowl with a spatula.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients.
5. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet until just combined.
6. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and bake 50 to 55 minutes, until the cake passes the toothpick test.
7. Remove the cake from the oven, transfer the pan to a wire rack, and cool 10 minutes.
8. Turn the cake out of the pan and place it right-side up on the rack, with a rimmed baking sheet underneath to catch the glaze.
9. Melt the remaining marmalade, then whisk in the confectioners' sugar and glaze butter until smooth and spread over the top of the cake.
10. Cool completely before slicing.
