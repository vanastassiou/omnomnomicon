_Makes 24 chouquettes._

### Make the choux paste

1. Set a rack in center of oven and preheat to 425°F (218°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment paper and lightly coat with vegetable oil spray.
2. In a medium saucepan, combine butter, granulated sugar, salt, and 120 mL water. Bring to a boil over medium heat, keeping a close eye.
3. Remove from heat, add flour, and stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until fully incorporated.
4. Return to very low heat and cook, stirring, about 1 minute, until dough is drier and no longer sticks to pan.
5. Transfer to a stand mixer fitted with paddle attachment. With mixer on low, add eggs one at a time, scraping down bowl after each, until fully incorporated, about 2 minutes total.

### Pipe and top

1. Transfer paste to a piping bag fitted with a medium round tip. (Or spoon it into a resealable plastic bag and snip off a bottom corner.)
2. Pipe 1-inch (2.5 cm) rounds, about 1 inch (2.5 cm) apart, onto prepared sheet pan.
3. Lightly brush each with a bit of milk, gently pressing to smooth any pointy tips. Sprinkle generously with pearl sugar.

### Bake

1. Transfer to oven, immediately reduce temperature to 375°F (190°C), and bake for 12 minutes.
2. Rotate pan as needed and bake until golden brown on top and hollow-feeling inside, 7 to 10 minutes more.
3. Cool on pan for at least 15 minutes, then serve warm or at room temperature.
