_Makes 24 cookies._

1. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, dry spices, and cocoa powder.
3. In the bowl of an electric mixer, beat the butter and grated ginger until lightened, about 4 minutes.
4. Add the brown sugar to the butter mixture and beat until combined.
5. Add the molasses to the butter mixture and beat until combined.
6. In a small bowl, dissolve the baking soda in the boiling water.
7. Beat half the flour mixture into the butter mixture, then add the baking-soda mixture, then add the remaining flour mixture.
8. Mix in the chocolate chunks.
9. Turn the dough out onto a piece of plastic wrap and pat it out to about 1 inch thick, then cover with more wrap.
10. Refrigerate the dough until firm, 2 hours or overnight.
11. Preheat the oven to 325°F (163°C).
12. Roll the dough into 1.5-inch balls and place them 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets, then refrigerate 20 minutes.
13. Roll the dough balls in granulated sugar and return them to the sheets.
14. Bake until the surfaces crack slightly, 10 to 12 minutes, rotating halfway through.
15. Let the cookies cool on the sheets 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack and let cool completely.

### Notes

- Cookies keep in an airtight container at room temperature up to 5 days.
- Add 1/4 cup finely chopped candied ginger for extra spice.
