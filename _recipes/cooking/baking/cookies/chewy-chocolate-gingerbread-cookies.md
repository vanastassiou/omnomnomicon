_Makes 24 cookies._

1. In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, spices, and cocoa powder.
2. In a large mixing bowl, beat the butter and grated ginger until fluffy.
3. With the mixer running, add the brown sugar and molasses and mix well.
4. In a small bowl, dissolve the baking soda in the boiling water.
5. Beat half the flour mixture into the butter mixture, then beat in the baking-soda solution, then beat in the remaining flour mixture.
6. Mix in the chocolate chunks.
7. Lay down a sheet of plastic wrap and flatten the dough into a 1-inch-thick sheet, then wrap well and refrigerate until firm.
8. Roll the dough into balls about 1.5 inches in diameter.
9. Preheat the oven to 325°F (163°C) and line two cookie sheets.
10. Roll the dough balls in granulated sugar, then arrange them on the cookie sheets about 2 inches apart.
11. Refrigerate the dough 20 minutes, then bake until the cookies have expanded and the surfaces are slightly cracked, about 10 minutes.
12. Let cool 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

### Notes

- Chill the cookie balls before they go into the oven, or you get cookie puddles.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature up to 5 days.
