_Makes 20 cookies._

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer, beat the butter and sugars until fluffy.
2. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract.
3. Separately, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
4. With the mixer running slowly, pour the dry ingredients into the mixer bowl.
5. Once the wet and dry are combined, add the milk and mix well.
6. Add any mix-ins, if using.
7. Wrap the dough tightly and chill at least 3 hours, up to 3 days.
8. When ready to bake, remove the dough from the refrigerator and let it sit at room temperature 10 minutes.
9. Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and line baking sheets.
10. Scoop and roll the dough into balls about 1.5 tablespoons each.
11. Bake 11 to 12 minutes, until the edges appear set and the centres still look soft.
12. Let cool 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack to cool completely.

### Notes

- Store the cookies in an airtight container at room temperature up to 1 week.
- Do not skip the chilling step, or you get crispy pancakes instead of cookies.
