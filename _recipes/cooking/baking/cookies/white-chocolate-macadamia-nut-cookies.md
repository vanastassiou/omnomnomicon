_Makes 30 cookies._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and line cookie sheets with parchment.
2. Melt the 115 g of white chocolate and add it to a mixer bowl.
3. Add the sugar, salt, baking powder, baking soda, nutmeg, and butter, and cream the mixture on medium speed until fluffy.
4. Mix in the egg and vanilla, then turn the speed to low and mix in the flour.
5. Fold in the macadamia nuts and the remaining white chocolate.
6. Scoop the dough in 2-tablespoon portions, arrange them on the cookie sheets 2 inches apart, and gently flatten each portion into a disk.
7. Bake until puffed and firm around the edges, about 15 minutes.
8. Cool directly on the baking sheet until the crumb is set, about 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack to finish cooling.

### Notes

- Choose a white chocolate that is about 30% cocoa butter: most of this recipe's flavour depends on it.
- These cookies will not brown as expected because of a relatively low lactose content. When fully baked, they will still be pale.
- The flavour is much better if you rest the dough a couple of hours in the fridge before baking. Freezing the dough to bake another day accomplishes the same thing.
- Store the finished cookies in an airtight container up to 3 days at room temperature.
