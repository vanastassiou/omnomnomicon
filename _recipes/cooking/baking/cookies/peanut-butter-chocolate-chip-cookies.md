_Makes 24 cookies._

1. In a medium bowl, sift together the flour, salt, baking powder, and baking soda.
2. In the bowl of a stand mixer, cream together the butter and sugars until pale and fluffy, about 2 minutes on medium speed.
3. Beat in the egg, vanilla extract, and peanut butter.
4. Add the dry ingredients from step 1 and mix until just combined.
5. Mix in the chocolate chips.
6. Seal the dough in an airtight container and chill in the refrigerator 2 hours.
7. Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
8. Scoop 1-ounce balls of dough (about 2 Tbsp) and place them on the prepared sheet 2 inches apart, then flatten slightly with the tines of a fork.
9. Bake until just brown on the edges, about 15 minutes.
10. Let cool on the pans 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack to finish.

### Notes

- Use bittersweet dark chocolate chips: the cookies are quite sweet, and semisweet chips will be too cloying.
