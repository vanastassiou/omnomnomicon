_Makes 30 cookies._

1. Add the butter to a saucepan and cook over medium heat until the solids have browned.
2. Remove from the heat and add the ice cube. Let cool completely.
3. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
4. In the bowl of a stand mixer, whip the eggs, granulated sugar, and vanilla until the mixture falls off the beater in thick ribbons, about 5 minutes.
5. Switch to the paddle attachment. Mix the cooled brown butter with the egg-sugar mixture until barely combined.
6. Add the flour mixture and mix until barely combined.
7. Add the chocolate and mix until the dough just comes together.
8. Seal the dough in an airtight container and refrigerate overnight.
9. Preheat the oven to 325°F (163°C) and line cookie sheets with parchment paper.
10. Scoop the dough in 1-ounce scoops (about 2 Tbsp) onto the pan, spacing them about 2 inches apart. Leave the dough balls rough and craggy for better texture.
11. Bake about 15 minutes, until golden brown around the edges.
12. Let the cookies cool on the pan 2 minutes, then transfer to a rack to finish cooling.

### Notes

- Cookies keep 5 days at room temperature in a sealed container, in theory.
- You do not have to rest the dough overnight, but the flavour definitely suffers if you skip it.
