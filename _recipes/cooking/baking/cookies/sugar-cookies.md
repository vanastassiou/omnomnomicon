_Makes 20 cookies._

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together and set aside.
2. In the bowl of a stand mixer, beat the butter and sugar until creamed.
3. Add the egg and vanilla extract and beat on high speed until combined.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix on low until combined.
5. Seal the dough in an airtight container and chill 2 hours, up to 4 days.
6. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and prepare baking sheets.
7. Scoop the dough into 2-Tbsp balls and place them 3 inches apart on the baking sheets.
8. Bake 12 to 13 minutes, until lightly browned on the sides.
9. Remove from the oven and let cool 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
