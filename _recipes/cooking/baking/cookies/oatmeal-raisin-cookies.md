_Makes about 30 cookies._

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer, cream the butter and sugars until smooth.
2. Add the eggs and mix on high until combined.
3. Add the vanilla and molasses and mix on high until combined.
4. Separately, combine the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet and mix on low until combined.
6. Beat in the oats and any mix-ins.
7. Seal the dough in an airtight container and chill 1 hour, up to 2 days.
8. Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and prepare two baking sheets.
9. When ready to bake, portion the dough into 2-Tbsp balls and place them 2 inches apart.
10. Bake 12 to 14 minutes, until lightly browned on the sides.
11. Let cool 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
