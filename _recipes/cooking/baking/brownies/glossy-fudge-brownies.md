_Makes one 9x13 pan, about 24 pieces._

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and line the pan with foil.
2. Brown the butter, then remove from the heat and stir in the dark chocolate.
3. Sift the flour and cocoa powder together.
4. Whip the sugars, salt, eggs, vanilla, and espresso until very thick and fluffy, about 8 minutes on medium-high mixer speed.
5. Reduce the mixer speed to low and add the butter mixture; mix well.
6. Add the cocoa and flour mixture all at once and combine roughly.
7. Finish mixing by hand with a spatula to reach the sides and bottom of the bowl.
8. Pour the batter into the pan and bake until glossy and just barely firm, about 30 minutes, or to an internal temperature of 205°F (96°C).
9. Cool to room temperature in the pan, then unmould and slice into 24 pieces.

### Notes

- Cooking time is based on an anodized aluminum pan; results vary significantly with glass, ceramic, or nonstick pans.
- Store up to 1 week at room temperature in an airtight container.
- If you burn the butter while browning it, strain out the solids before use.
