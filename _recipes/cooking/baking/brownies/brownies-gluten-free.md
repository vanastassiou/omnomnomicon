_Makes one 9x9 pan._

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F (160°C), then grease and line the baking pan.
2. Whisk the two cocoas together in a heatproof mixing bowl.
3. In a saucepan, heat the oil to 240°F (115°C).
4. Pour the hot oil over the cocoa and mix well, then let it sit 10 minutes.
5. Add the salt, baking powder, and egg whites, and beat until fully blended.
6. Add the remaining ingredients and beat until smooth and lightened.
7. Transfer the batter to the prepared pan and smooth the top.
8. Bake about 30 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the middle comes out with some moist crumbs attached.
9. Let cool completely before cutting.
