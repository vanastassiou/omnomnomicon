_Makes about 180 mL._

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix well.

### Notes

- Refrigerate up to 1 week.
- Use as a dipping sauce or glaze for chicken, especially fried, or toss with crispy tofu or stir-fried vegetables in the last few seconds of cooking.
