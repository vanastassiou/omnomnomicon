_Makes about 300 mL._

1. Brown the butter in a saucepan until it smells nutty, then transfer it to a heatproof bowl.
2. Add the garlic and let it cook in the residual heat for a minute.
3. Whisk in the hot sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, salt, and pepper vigorously to emulsify.

### Notes

- Refrigerate up to 1 week.
