_Makes about 300 mL._

1. Combine the oil, dried onion, half the sugar, and half the salt in a small saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat until the onion toasts evenly.
3. Add the chiles, sesame seeds, and Sichuan peppercorns; cook 1 minute.
4. Stir in the remaining sugar and salt.

### Notes

- Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.
