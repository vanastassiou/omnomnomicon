_Makes about 300 mL, enough for one chili recipe._

1. Tear the dried peppers into small pieces.
2. Toast the whole spices and pepper pieces in a dry frying pan until fragrant.
3. Heat the stock to a simmer in a saucepan, then add the toasted contents of the frying pan and let sit 15 minutes.
4. Combine the steeped mixture with the remaining ingredients and blend to a smooth paste with a blender, food processor, or immersion blender.

### Notes

- You can make this in advance and freeze it for later.
