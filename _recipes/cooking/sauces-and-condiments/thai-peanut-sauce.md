_Makes about 360 mL._

1. Add all ingredients to a food processor and blend until smooth. Use 1 to 2 garlic cloves and 1 to 2 Tbsp fish sauce to taste.

### Notes

- Substitute 180 g (about 3/4 cup) peanut butter if you have no peanuts.
- Swap the fish sauce for 22 to 37 mL soy sauce, the tamarind paste for 7.5 mL lime juice, or the cayenne for 5 mL Thai chile sauce or sriracha.
