_Makes about 1.5 litres._

1. Combine everything except the rum and sugar cubes in a non-reactive saucepan.
2. Cover and heat until gently steaming; do not let it boil.
3. Hold on the lowest setting for 45 minutes, or longer if needed.
4. When ready to serve, set a stainless steel rack or colander over the pot and arrange the sugar cubes on top.
5. Soak the sugar cubes with rum, using anywhere from 120 to 240 mL depending on how much flame you want.
6. Light the rum and let the sugar melt into the wine, ladling more rum over the cubes to keep them melting until they are gone.

### Notes

- For extra authenticity, order a Zuckerhut (sugar loaf) from a German online grocery and use the dedicated stainless steel holder for it.
- A coarse stainless steel strainer also works to hold the sugar cubes over the pot.
