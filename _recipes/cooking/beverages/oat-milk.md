_Makes about 1 litre._

1. Combine the oats, water, salt, and any optional add-ins in a high-speed blender. Start with 710 mL of water and add up to 950 mL for a thinner milk.
2. Blend 30 seconds to 1 minute, until well combined but not fully pulverized.
3. Taste and adjust the flavour, adding sweetener, spices, cocoa powder, or berries as desired.
4. Line a bowl with finely woven cloth, pour in the blender contents, then gather and suspend the cloth to let the milk drip through.
5. Transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate up to 5 days.

### Notes

- No blender? An immersion blender works well.
- For a cream-like texture, heat the finished oat milk to boiling, then let it cool.
- Other kinds of oats work, but the coarser the grind, the more pulp is left behind.
- Ways to use up the leftover pulp: porridge, bread dough, cookie dough, muffin batter, pancake batter, meatloaf, smoothies, a binder for sausage, or waffles.
