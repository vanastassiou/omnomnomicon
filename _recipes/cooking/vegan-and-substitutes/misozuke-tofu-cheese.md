_Makes one 350 g block of tofu cheese._

1. Press the tofu gently under a light weight to drain some water, about 1 hour.
2. Cover a plate with a large piece of clean, dry muslin.
3. Mix the rice syrup, mirin, lemon zest, kombu powder, and both misos into a smooth paste.
4. Smear the miso mixture over the entire surface of the tofu, leaving no gaps.
5. Wrap the tofu in the muslin and leave at room temperature 3 to 5 days.
6. Unwrap the tofu and scrape off the miso layer.
7. Store the tofu in an airtight container in the fridge up to 1 week.

### Notes

- If you can buy pressed tofu, use it to save time.
- Keep the scraped miso in a sealed container in the fridge to reuse for this recipe.
