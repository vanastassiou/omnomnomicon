_Serves 8 as an appetizer._

1. Trim and slice the mushrooms.
2. Heat the olive oil and butter in a skillet, add the onion and garlic, and cook until translucent and fragrant.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook until well browned.
4. Combine the mushroom mixture with all the remaining ingredients in a food processor and process until completely smooth.
5. Taste and balance with more salt, pepper, Cognac, soy sauce, or lemon juice as needed.
6. Scrape into a serving bowl and refrigerate a few hours, until firm.

### Notes

- To make it vegan, substitute more olive oil for the butter.
- To make it gluten free, substitute tamari for the soy sauce.
- One cup of dried lentils yields the 400 g of cooked lentils you need.
- Well wrapped, this keeps 4 days in the refrigerator or up to 2 months in the freezer.
