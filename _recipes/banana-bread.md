---
layout: recipe
title: Banana Bread
date: 2026-01-02
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - amount: 250
    unit: g
    name: flour
  - amount: 3
    unit: count
    name: ripe bananas
  - amount: 2
    unit: count
    name: eggs
  - amount: 150
    unit: g
    name: sugar
  - amount: 100
    unit: g
    name: butter, melted
---

1. Heat the oven to 175&deg;C and line a loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas, then whisk in the eggs, sugar, and melted butter.
3. Fold in the flour until just combined.
4. Pour into the tin and bake 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
