_Makes 12 canelés._

### Make the batter

1. In a small saucepan, combine the milk, ¾ cup (136 g) of the sugar, and the butter. Scrape in the vanilla seeds and add the pod.
2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, keeping a close eye.
3. Remove from the heat, add the flour, and stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until fully incorporated.
4. Return to very low heat and cook, stirring, about 1 minute, until the dough is drier and no longer sticks to the pan.
5. Meanwhile, in a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, whip the egg yolks, ⅔ cup (136 g) of the sugar, the rum, and the salt on medium until pale yellow, about 2 minutes.
6. Discard the vanilla pod from the milk mixture. With the mixer on low, slowly add the hot milk and whip until fully combined, 2 to 3 minutes.
7. Gradually add the flour in 4 batches, whipping until fully combined after each addition, about 4 minutes total.
8. Pour the batter through a sieve into a 4-cup (960 ml) spouted measuring cup.

### Chill

1. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 24 hours and up to 48 hours.

### Bake

1. Set a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 450°F (230°C). Coat a canelé mold with vegetable oil spray, using a pastry brush to cover all the crevices.
2. Remove the batter from the refrigerator, give it a stir, and fill each mold about three-quarters of the way.
3. Bake for 10 minutes, then reduce the oven to 375°F (190°C), rotate as needed, and continue baking until the tops are golden brown, 30 to 35 minutes.
4. With a small knife, remove each canelé from the pan and set on a wire rack to cool for about 30 minutes.
